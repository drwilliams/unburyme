unbury.me

Get unburied from debt.

unbury.me helps you get debt-free as fast as possible. Enter your loans, then set the total amount you can put towards them each month. Anything above the minimum payments goes to one loan at a time until it's gone. Once a loan is paid off, the money that was going to it rolls over to the next one, and so on.

Loans are paid off either from the highest interest rate down (Avalanche) or from the smallest balance up (Snowball).

Try putting an extra $100 a month towards your loans and see how much time and interest you save.
